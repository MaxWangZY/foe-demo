How to read the demo

Timeline. Each policy day is one step of the numerical best-response search. Intermediate profiles can violate obedience. The status badge reports the numerical checks for the final result.

Traffic. The diagram shows mean traffic across the mediators’ possible route allocations. More particles indicate more flow, and their speed is illustrative. Select a mediator to highlight its traffic and see its contribution to each edge. Edge widths and costs use the total mean flow.

Costs. The chart averages total travel cost across policy draws for all travelers or the selected mediator’s group. The benchmark tabs show network-wide flows. The social optimum gives a lower bound on total cost. Wardrop is a reference for selfish routing.

Policies and exports. For verified cases, the Policies tab shows the recovered final allocations and their probabilities. Full numerical checks are included in run exports.

For the formal model and numerical method, see Fragmented Obedient Equilibria in Routing Games.
